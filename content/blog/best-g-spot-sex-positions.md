In plain missionary, the angle often runs straight along the vaginal canal. The tip of the penis or toy glides past the front wall without pressing into it much, so straight-on thrusting can miss the G-spot.

To reach it, the tip needs to press toward the front wall, the side toward her belly button.

### What position hits the G-spot best?

Leapfrog and Pillow Driver often give the strongest G-spot angles. In leapfrog, her chest is down and hips are up, which tilts the front wall toward the penis or toy. You'll usually feel a deep, full, pressure-like pleasure on the front wall, the side toward your belly button. It may feel different from clit pleasure, more of a building ache than a sharp tingle. Peeing before sex helps you relax into it. Sometimes that feeling comes right before squirting. If you ever have pain or burning when you pee, see a doctor.

### Is the G-spot real?

Researchers still argue about whether the G-spot is its own separate structure. Many experts think it's the area where the inner clitoris, the urethra, and the urethral sponge meet the front wall of the vagina. Either way, lots of women feel real pleasure from pressure there. So the area is real, even if the name makes it sound like a single button.
