## Why Height Matters (Mostly When You're Standing)

## The Level-Up Tricks

The simple rule: the shorter partner goes up, or the taller partner goes down. Here are the easiest ways to do it.

### Use the bed

The [edge of the bed](/define/edge-of-the-bed/) turns your mattress into a height tool. She lies or kneels on the bed, and her partner kneels or stands on the floor. Now bed height decides where her hips sit, and legs stop mattering.

### Find some stairs

Every step is a built-in height change. The shorter partner stands or kneels one step up, and you can fine-tune by moving a step at a time.

### Try a table or counter

A sturdy table or kitchen counter lifts her hips to his height when he's tall. Test it first by leaning your weight on it. Throw a folded towel on top for comfort.

### Stack pillows

Pillows raise hips in lying and kneeling positions. A firm wedge pillow [[AFF: wedge pillow]] holds its shape much better than a regular pillow.

### Grab a step stool

A sturdy step stool gives the shorter partner a few extra inches for standing positions. Pick one that's wide, flat, and non-slip, with a weight rating that covers you.
