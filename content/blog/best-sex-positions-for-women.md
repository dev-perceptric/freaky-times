### How to read the score cards

Each position has five meters, each out of 5 dots. The first four are the Pleasure Score parts, scaled down to five dots so they're quick to scan. The fifth meter, **Effort**, shows how much work the position takes. More dots means more work. Effort doesn't count toward the ranking, but it helps you pick something for tonight.

The dots are rounded, so two positions with similar meters can land a few spots apart.

You'll notice a pattern. Positions with easy clit contact rise to the top.
