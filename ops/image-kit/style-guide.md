# FreakyTimes Image Style Guide
**Version 1.0.** Use this for every image on the site, social media, and Pinterest.

---

## 1. The look in one line
**Flat, playful, faceless figures in coral and plum on a warm cream background.** It's sexy and fun without being explicit. Think modern sex-ed poster meets a cute app.

### Why this style
- **Safe everywhere:** Google, Pinterest, TikTok, and ad networks accept it. AI image tools are much less likely to block it.
- **Cheap and fast:** Flat shapes are easy for cheap AI models to get right.
- **Inclusive by default:** Figures come in brand colors and many body shapes, so every reader can see themselves.
- **Instantly ours:** Kinkly uses stock photos and mixed art. One clear style makes FreakyTimes easy to recognize.

## 2. Colors

### Main palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Freaky Coral** | `#FF6B6B` | Figure A (usually the woman, or the partner the article is about) |
| **Deep Plum** | `#5B2A86` | Figure B (the partner) |
| **Honey** | `#F4B740` | Figure C (third person), and all toys and props that need to pop |
| **Cream** | `#FFF6EC` | Background on every image |
| **Lavender** | `#E6DDF5` | Beds, couches, chairs, walls, furniture |
| **Blush** | `#FFD6D0` | Pillows, blankets, wedges, soft props |
| **Ink** | `#2B1B3D` | Shadows (at 10–15% opacity), small details, all hair |
| **Peach** | `#F8DCCB` | Blank face ovals only |

### Color rules
- The background is **always Cream**, with no gradients or patterns.
- **Figures never use realistic skin colors.** They're always Coral, Plum, or Honey.
- When figures overlap, the **front figure** is Coral and the **back figure** is Plum, so they never blend together.
- **Toys are always Honey**, drawn as simple rounded shapes. Only mention a toy in the prompt when the pose uses one. A vague "toy" line makes AI draw kids' toys like teddy bears, which must never appear.
- No pure black and no pure white. Use Ink and Cream instead.
- About 60% cream, 30% figures, 10% furniture and props.

## 3. Figures

### Body
- **Smooth, rounded, simple shapes.** Limbs are soft tubes, with gentle curves at the hips, chest, and bum.
- **Faceless.** Each face is a blank pale peach oval (`#F8DCCB`) with no eyes, nose, mouth, or ears. The hair frames it.
- **Mitten hands.** Simple rounded hands, no fingers drawn (fingers are where AI makes the most mistakes).
- **Simple feet.** Rounded shapes.
- **Nothing explicit, ever:** no genitals, no nipples, no fluids. Bodies smoothly merge where they touch.
- **Clearly adult bodies:** adult proportions, adult hips and shoulders. Never childlike shapes or school-style props.

### Hair (gives each figure personality)
Use one hair shape per figure, in Ink or a darker shade of the figure color:
- High bun
- Long straight hair
- Big curls / afro
- Short bob
- Buzz cut / short crop
- Ponytail
- Braids

Mix hairstyles across the site so it doesn't feel like the same couple every time.

### Body types
- **Default:** slender and toned, like fashion illustration figures. The woman has an hourglass silhouette, a narrow waist, and long slim legs. The man is lean and athletic with broad shoulders. Prompt words: "slender," "toned," "elegant," "fashion illustration." Avoid "sexy" and "bare," which trigger the filter.
- **Solid-color bodies:** each body is one smooth flat color (the "bodysuit" look). This is what Gemini's filter allows for sex poses. Prompts asking for bare bodies get blocked.
- **Plus-size:** used only in the plus-size article, where it's the point of the page.
- **Tall and short pair:** used only in the height difference article.

### Couples
| Article type | Figure A | Figure B |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Woman (Coral) | Man (Plum) |
| Lesbian | Woman (Coral) | Woman (Plum) |
| Strap-on | Receiver (Coral) | Wearer (Plum) in a simple harness |
| Group | Coral | Plum, plus Honey for the third |

- **Strap-on harness:** drawn as smooth Ink straps. The toy stays hidden by the angle.
- **Men vs. women** are shown by body shape and hair only: broader shoulders and a straighter shape, or curved hips and chest shape.

## 4. Composition

### Position drawings (the main image type)
- **Size:** 1200 × 1200 (square)
- **Camera:** side view, at the level of the bed. Use a slight top-down view only when the side view hides the pose (spooning, 69).
- **Framing:** figures centered, filling about 70% of the width, with at least 10% empty space on every side.
- **Furniture:** one simple shape only (a bed as a lavender rounded rectangle, a chair, a table, or stairs). No full rooms.
- **Props:** at most 2 (a pillow, a wedge, or a toy).
- **Shadow:** one soft Ink oval under the furniture, at 10–15% opacity.
- **No text, logos, or watermarks** inside the image. Ever.

### Hero images (top of each article)
- **Size:** 1600 × 900
- **Content:** clothed figures (cuddling, laughing, talking) or an abstract shape. No sex poses in heroes, so they're safe for social sharing and link previews.
- **Layout:** keep the left 40% fairly empty so a title can sit over it on social cards.

### Pinterest pins
- **Size:** 1000 × 1500
- **Made in Canva**, never fully AI. Layout: hero art at the top, the title in the brand font, 3–4 round position icons, and the logo at the bottom.

### Position icons
- **Size:** 256 × 256, a circle on a Blush background
- Simplified version of the drawing, cropped tight

### Diagrams and charts
- **Made in Figma or Canva, never AI.** AI gets anatomy and text wrong.
- Same colors, rounded shapes, and labels in the brand font
- Anatomy diagrams stay soft and abstract, like a gentle textbook drawing

### Product photos (toy reviews)
- **Always real photos we take ourselves.** AI product photos are fake reviews, which break FTC rules and lose reader trust.
- Cream or blush paper backdrop, soft window light, shot from above (flat lay) or at 45°
- One Honey or Lavender prop (a flower, a ribbon, a lube bottle)

## 6. Do and don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Flat colors, smooth shapes | Gradients, 3D, shiny or realistic rendering |
| Faceless oval heads | Eyes, mouths, facial expressions |
| Mitten hands | Detailed fingers |
| Cream background | White, black, or patterned backgrounds |
| Coral front figure, Plum back figure | Two figures in the same color |
| Honey toys, simple shapes | Realistic or detailed toys |
| Adult bodies of many shapes | Childlike bodies, school uniforms |
| One furniture piece | Full bedrooms, windows, lamps |
| No text in AI images | AI-made words or labels |
| Real photos for products | AI-made product photos |

## 7. The master prompt
Copy this exactly. Only change the **[POSE]**, **[BODIES]**, and **[FURNITURE/PROPS]** parts.

```
Flat vector illustration in a minimal modern health-education style.
[POSE].
[BODIES].
Each figure's body, neck, arms, hands, legs, and feet are ONE single flat solid color, like a cut-paper silhouette.
No skin tones on the body. No clothing details.
Figures are smooth, rounded, simplified shapes. Each face is a blank pale peach #F8DCCB oval with no features
(no eyes, no mouth, no ears drawn).
Mitten hands with no fingers, simple rounded feet. Nothing explicit: no genitals, no nipples.
Bodies merge smoothly where they touch. Clearly adult proportions.
Front figure is solid coral #FF6B6B, back figure is solid deep plum #5B2A86. Hair is simple solid shapes in dark ink #2B1B3D.
[FURNITURE/PROPS] drawn as simple flat shapes in lavender #E6DDF5, pillows in blush #FFD6D0.
[ONLY IF THE POSE HAS A TOY: The small vibrator is a simple smooth rounded honey #F4B740 shape.]
No other objects: no stuffed animals, no plants, no decorations.
Plain solid cream #FFF6EC background. One soft ink shadow under the furniture at low opacity.
Side view, centered, figures fill about 70% of the frame with empty space around them.
Flat colors only, no gradients, no outlines, no texture, no text, no logos, no watermark. Square 1:1.
```

### [BODIES] options

## 8. Keeping every image consistent

**Approved style anchor:** `images/style-anchor.png` (Cowgirl). Attach it to every prompt.

AI tools drift. These tricks keep 100+ images looking like one set:
1. **Make a "style anchor" first.** Generate Cowgirl until one looks perfect. Save it as `style-anchor.png`.
2. **Always attach the anchor as a reference image** and add: *"Match the exact style, colors, and shapes of the reference image. Only change the pose."* Gemini's image models accept a reference image with the prompt.
3. **Same prompt every time.** Only swap the bracketed parts.
4. **Fix colors after.** If a color is a little off, recolor it to the exact hex in Canva, Figma, Photopea (free), or by vectorizing it.
5. **Review all drawings side by side** in one grid before publishing. Redo any that look off.

## 9. Cheap ways to make the images

| Tool | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini Flash image models via the API** (`gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`, `gemini-2.5-flash-image`) | Cheapest paid option, a few cents or less per image | Already connected. Use the **Batch API** for big runs, which cuts the price about in half. Use Flash Lite first, and only use the bigger Pro image model to redo hard poses. Check Google's current pricing before a big run. |
| **Google AI Studio** (website) | Free daily limit | Good for testing the style anchor by hand |
| **Recraft** | Free tier, cheap paid plans | Makes true vector (SVG) art, which fits this style well |
| **Canva** | Free | Pins, icons, charts, recoloring, text |
| **Figma** | Free | Diagrams, charts, icon sets |
| **Photopea** | Free | Quick color fixes and cropping |
| **Squoosh** (squoosh.app) | Free | Convert to WebP and shrink file size |
| **Freelance illustrator** | $15–$60 per drawing | Backup for poses AI blocks or gets wrong. Send them this guide and the style anchor. |

**Budget plan for the 91 position drawings:**
- Generate with Gemini Flash Lite through the Batch API
- Expect about 2–3 tries per good image, so about 250 generations
- That should cost only a few dollars. Check pricing first.

### Content filter tips
- Keep words clinical and calm: "lying on her back," "kneeling behind," "straddling."
- Avoid slang in prompts (no "doggy," "riding," "sex"). Describe body placement only.
- If a pose keeps getting blocked, try the clothed version (figures in simple solid-color underwear shapes) or send it to an illustrator.

## 10. Files and naming
| Type | Name format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Position drawing | `position-[slug].webp` | `position-reverse-cowgirl.webp` |
| Variant | `position-[slug]-[variant].webp` | `position-spooning-two-women.webp` |
| Hero | `hero-[article-slug].webp` | `hero-best-g-spot-sex-positions.webp` |
| Pin | `pin-[article-slug].webp` | `pin-best-69-variations.webp` |
| Icon | `icon-[slug].webp` | `icon-cowgirl.webp` |
| Diagram | `diagram-[name].webp` | `diagram-g-spot-location.webp` |
| Product photo | `photo-[brand]-[product]-[n].webp` | `photo-dame-pom-1.webp` |

- **Format:** WebP, quality 80–85
- **Max file size:** 150 KB for drawings, 250 KB for heroes
- **Keep the original PNG** in a "masters" folder
- **Alt text** is required for every image (it's in the image CSV). Describe what it shows: "Illustration of the Reverse Cowgirl sex position."

## 11. Quality checklist (check every image)
- [ ] Pose matches the description (right person on top, right direction)
- [ ] Correct number of arms, legs, and heads
- [ ] No faces, no fingers, nothing explicit
- [ ] Bodies look clearly adult
- [ ] Coral front, Plum back, Honey toys
- [ ] Cream background, one furniture piece
- [ ] No text, letters, or watermark
- [ ] Matches the style anchor when placed next to it
- [ ] Right size, WebP, under the file size limit
- [ ] Named correctly, with alt text added

## 12. Hard rules
- Never draw or edit images of **real people** (no celebrities, no one you know, no photo uploads).
- Never make anything that looks like a **minor**.
- Never use AI for **product photos** or **"real customer" images**.
- Never put **text** made by AI on an image.
